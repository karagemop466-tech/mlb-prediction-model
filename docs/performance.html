Forward test (live, un-fakeable)

Predictions are timestamped and committed before first pitch, then graded after. Unlike a backtest, this cannot be corrupted by hindsight.

15 graded — accuracy 60.0% vs backtest expectation 56.8%. Sample far too small to be meaningful (±24.8% at 95% confidence); ~300 games are needed before this says anything.
